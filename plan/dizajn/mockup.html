Limits, in full

  • Market value is a community estimate and lags behind form.
  • Defenders and goalkeepers: rated on role and team context, not their own actions.
  • Regional leagues: two seasons of data; the model is applied there, not backtested.
  • Under 900 minutes a rating can move a lot.
  • Edge over "most minutes" is within the margin of error.
Data: open Transfermarkt dataset · 23 leagues · 2012–2026 · Blind pick, player card and budget radar show invented samples · Time machine and Bosnian-passport list are real output of the prototype model
